# Open Questions & Architectural Decisions (OPEN_QUESTIONS.md)

This document tracks unresolved product decisions, tenant configuration variations, and confirmed architectural choices across the ERP multi-tenant platform.

---

## 1. Pending Decisions (Awaiting User Resolution)

*None. All pending baseline architectural, domain, and localization decisions have been formally resolved, confirmed, and codified across `docs/PRD.md`, `docs/DATA_MODEL.md`, `docs/PERMISSIONS.md`, `docs/SCREENS.md`, and `docs/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`.*

## 2. Confirmed Architectural Decisions

The following architectural choices have been reviewed, approved, and codified:

| # | Topic | Confirmed Decision | Implementation Location |
| :-: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | **Fee Ledger Scoping & Rollover** | `fee_ledger_entries` references `enrollment_id`. When a session closes, net unpaid arrears or overpaid advance credits roll into the new enrollment as a single signed `opening_balance` entry. | `docs/PRD.md` Sec 3.3, `docs/DATA_MODEL.md` Sec 3.9 |
| 2 | **Sibling Discount Scope** | Applies strictly to younger siblings sharing a `guardian_id` via `student_guardians` (eldest by DOB ascending pays full fee). Defaults exclusively to tuition heads. | `docs/PRD.md` Sec 3.2, `docs/DATA_MODEL.md` Sec 3.4 & Sec 3.9 |
| 3 | **Monetary Rounding Standard** | Half-up rounding away from zero ($\operatorname{sgn}(x) \times \lfloor \|x\| + 0.5 \rfloor$) computed per line item to integer minor units prior to summation. | `docs/PRD.md` Sec 3.8, `docs/DATA_MODEL.md` Sec 1.3 |
| 4 | **Late Fine Typology** | Exactly two non-compounding types: `fixed_amount` or `per_day`, with optional cap (`max_fine_minor_units`) and grace period (`grace_period_days`). | `docs/PRD.md` Sec 3.1, `docs/DATA_MODEL.md` Sec 3.9 |
| 5 | **Class Ranking Mode** | Default is `rank_mode = 'shared'` with Standard Competition Ranking (`1, 2, 2, 4`), with alternative `rank_mode = 'tie_break'` executing a 4-stage deterministic tie-breaker. | `docs/PRD.md` Sec 3.6, `docs/DATA_MODEL.md` Sec 3.8 |
| 6 | **Job Queue Architecture** | Native PostgreSQL `job_queue` table with atomic row-level dequeuing (`FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`) processed by `pg_cron` and Supabase Edge Functions. | `docs/DATA_MODEL.md` Sec 3.24 |
| 7 | **Accounts Module Scope (V1)** | Pragmatic single-entry cash book, income/expense heads, cash/bank accounts, daily cash closing, and P&L. No double-entry general ledger or journal vouchers in V1. | `docs/PRD.md` Module 8, `docs/DATA_MODEL.md` Sec 3.10 |
| 8 | **Payment Gateway Decoupling** | Pluggable interface (`PaymentGatewayAdapter`) supporting Stripe, Razorpay, or local banking gateways. | `docs/PRD.md` Sec 1.2, `docs/DATA_MODEL.md` Sec 3.23 |
| 9 | **Advance Payments & Overpayment** | Ledger entry amount is signed (`amount bigint`). Excess payments yield negative ledger balance. On next voucher, credit is allocated via `fee_payment_allocations` and displayed on the voucher without appending duplicate negative ledger entries. | `docs/PRD.md` Sec 3.9, `docs/DATA_MODEL.md` Sec 3.9 |
| 10 | **Student Evaluations (Absent vs Exempt)** | `exam_marks.marks_obtained` is nullable: NULL for absent (counted as 0 in totals, full max marks in denominator) and NULL for exempt (completely excluded from both numerator and denominator). | `docs/PRD.md` Module 6, `docs/DATA_MODEL.md` Sec 3.8 |
| 11 | **Late Fine Grace Period Mode (`grace_fine_mode`)** | Configured via `grace_fine_mode` with values `'incremental'` (**Default**) and `'retroactive'`. Stored on `fee_late_fine_policies` and snapshotted on each voucher together with the other fine parameters. Incremental mode charges only days after grace; retroactive mode charges all days since due date once grace is exceeded. | `docs/PRD.md` Sec 3.1, `docs/DATA_MODEL.md` Sec 3.9 |
| 12 | **India Localization & Compliance Configuration** | India defaults: Currency pre-selected as `INR` (locked after first voucher), timezone `Asia/Kolkata`, date format `DD/MM/YYYY`, number formatting `en-IN`, academic session starting in April. Educational board presets (`CBSE`, `ICSE`, `State`, `Custom`) seeding 33% pass mark for CBSE/State. Fee frequencies include `quarterly` and `half_yearly`. Social categories (`General`, `OBC`, `SC`, `ST`, `EWS`) and 100% RTE waiver discount type. Student identifiers (`sr_number`, `pen`, `apaar_id`, optional encrypted/masked Aadhaar). Name standard (`full_name` required, `last_name` optional across users/guardians/students/employees), Indian 10-digit mobile (+91), PIN code and State. Payment modes add `upi`, `net_banking`, `card`, `demand_draft` with dynamic UPI QR and direct split settlement to school bank accounts (zero platform fund custody). TRAI DLT-compliant SMS templates with push notifications primary and SMS fallback (console adapter for dev). DPDP Act 2023 privacy compliance (no ad/3rd-party analytics with PII, parental consent, per-school retention, export/erasure, `data_breach_logs` table with 72-hour incident runbook, DPA onboarding flow). Languages: English first, Hindi next, with Noto Sans Devanagari typography. | `docs/PRD.md` Sec 1.2, Sec 2, Sec 4, `docs/DATA_MODEL.md` Sec 1.1, Sec 3.1, Sec 3.4, Sec 3.9, Sec 3.10, Sec 3.12, Sec 3.23, Sec 3.27 |
| 13 | **Pass Mark Wizard & Hierarchy (Decision 1.1)** | School setup wizard explicitly requests the passing mark. Board presets suggest `33.00%` for CBSE and State presets; ICSE and Custom presets remain blank until confirmed by the school administrator. Hierarchy: school $\to$ class $\to$ subject, where lower levels inherit from higher levels when NULL (`subjects.passing_percentage` and `exam_schedules.passing_marks` default to NULL). | `docs/PRD.md` Sec 3.5, Module 1, `docs/DATA_MODEL.md` Sec 3.2, Sec 3.8, Sec 3.22 |
| 14 | **Mid-Month Admission Proration Rule (Decision 1.2)** | Configured via `school_settings.mid_month_admission_rule` supporting three options: `'full_month'` (**Default**, full month billed regardless of admission date), `'half_after_cutoff_day'` (50% tuition billed if admitted after cutoff day, default day 15 configured via `mid_month_admission_cutoff_day`), and `'daily_prorata'` (continuous daily pro-rata based on calendar days remaining in admission month). Full one-time charges apply in all modes without proration. | `docs/PRD.md` Module 7.4 (Edge Case 5), `docs/DATA_MODEL.md` Sec 3.22 |
| 15 | **Voucher Calendar Due Day (Decision 1.3)** | Default calendar due day is `10` of each month, configured per fee structure via `fee_structures.due_day_of_month` (with school-level fallback in `school_settings.due_day_of_month`). | `docs/PRD.md` Sec 3.1, Module 7.3, `docs/DATA_MODEL.md` Sec 3.9, Sec 3.22 |
| 16 | **Automated Absence Alert Timing & Hierarchy (Decision 1.4)** | Absence alerts are dispatched per class section exactly 10 minutes (`school_settings.absence_alert_offset_minutes`) after the teacher submits attendance for that class. If a class has no submission by the school's morning cutoff time (default `09:30`, `school_settings.absence_alert_cutoff_time`), urgent reminders are sent to the teacher and admin, and **never to parents**. No absence alerts are dispatched on designated school holidays. Multi-channel delivery: Mobile Push notification is primary; SMS is secondary fallback via registered TRAI DLT template. | `docs/PRD.md` Module 4.3, Module 10.3, `docs/DATA_MODEL.md` Sec 3.22 |
| 17 | **Working Days & Attendance Basis (Decision 1.5)** | Configured via `school_settings.week_pattern` (default Monday to Saturday, 6 working days) combined with the institutional holiday calendar. Student attendance percentage is computed strictly against real scheduled working days. | `docs/PRD.md` Module 4.3, `docs/DATA_MODEL.md` Sec 3.22 |
| 18 | **Grading Scale Presets (Decision 1.6)** | Configured via `school_settings.grading_preset` supporting: `standard_4_0` (6-tier letter scale with 4.0 weighted GPA), `percentage_only` (raw marks and percentages with zero letter grades or GPA), and `cbse_style` (**Default for India**, continuous evaluation bands marked "to be copied from the pilot school's report card"). All grade tiers and descriptors are fully editable. | `docs/PRD.md` Sec 3.5, `docs/DATA_MODEL.md` Sec 3.8, Sec 3.22 |
| 19 | **Currency Selection & Locking (Decision 1.7)** | Currency is pre-selected as `INR` for Indian schools, required at school creation, and permanently locked upon generation of the first fee voucher or insertion of the first fee ledger entry. No other fallback. All numerical worked examples are strictly expressed in Rupees and paise. | `docs/PRD.md` Sec 1.2, Sec 3.1, Sec 3.2, Sec 3.3, Sec 3.4, Sec 3.8, Sec 3.9, Module 7.3, `docs/DATA_MODEL.md` Sec 1.1, Sec 3.1, Sec 3.22 |
| 20 | **Promotion Attendance Floor (Decision 1.8)** | Minimum cumulative attendance percentage required for standard annual promotion without trial is `75.00%`, configurable per school via `school_settings.promotion_attendance_threshold`. | `docs/PRD.md` Sec 3.7, Module 4.3, `docs/DATA_MODEL.md` Sec 3.22 |
| 21 | **Library Borrowing Policy (Decision 1.9, P2)** | Standard institutional borrowing limits accepted as per-school settings (P2): default loan duration `14 calendar days` (`school_settings.library_loan_period_days`) and concurrent checkout limit `3 books per student` (`school_settings.library_max_books_per_student`). Overdue fine is 100 paise (₹1.00) per day (`school_settings.library_daily_late_fine_minor_units`). | `docs/PRD.md` Module 12.3, `docs/DATA_MODEL.md` Sec 3.14, Sec 3.22 |
| 22 | **Hostel Evening Curfew Policy (Decision 1.10, P2)** | Standard evening gate pass curfew cutoff time accepted as a per-school setting (P2): default `20:00 (8:00 PM)` local school time (`school_settings.hostel_curfew_time`). | `docs/PRD.md` Module 15.3, `docs/DATA_MODEL.md` Sec 3.17, Sec 3.22 |
| 23 | **Mandatory Explicit Selection for `retention_years` (Decision 1, Option 3)** | `school_settings.retention_years` has no platform default. The school onboarding wizard requires each school to explicitly select its record retention period in years, and onboarding cannot be completed without it. Note: A lawyer or chartered accountant must confirm any suggested preset before we add one. (Removed claim that 7 years is a statutory benchmark). Once the configured retention period expires, personal identifiers are permanently anonymised, while statutory financial receipts, ledger entries, attendance registers, and exam marks remain perpetual and intact. | `docs/PRD.md` Sec 1.2, Sec 21, Sec 24, `docs/DATA_MODEL.md` Sec 3.1, Sec 3.22, `docs/SCREENS.md` Screen 0.9, Screen 22.2 |
